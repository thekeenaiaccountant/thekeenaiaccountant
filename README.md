# The Keen AI Accountant

**Applied Finance Systems | Controls | Decision Intelligence**

I am an Australian Chartered Accountant working at the intersection of finance, systems and AI. I develop practical reference implementations, challenge their assumptions, and document the controls required for reliable financial use.

## What I focus on

- Finance automation grounded in accounting logic
- Reconciliations, exception handling, approvals, and audit trails
- Responsible AI use where evidence and accountability matter
- Clear financial analysis that supports better decisions

## Portfolio standard

Every public project begins with a fictional scenario and explains:

1. The finance problem and intended decision
2. The approach, assumptions, and data boundaries
3. The controls and failure modes
4. How the output is verified
5. Where professional judgement remains necessary

## Public data boundary

Everything published here is built from scratch with synthetic data. I do not publish client or employer names, data, documents, screenshots, account details, transaction records, credentials, configurations, or identifiable workflows.

## How I work

1. Define the finance question and decision clearly.
2. Design the workflow with privacy and control boundaries.
3. Reconcile the inputs, logic, and outputs.
4. Surface exceptions, assumptions, and limitations.
5. Keep people accountable for judgement and approval.

I publish the reasoning behind the work, including where automation should stop and professional judgement should take over.

> Public reference material only. Nothing here is accounting, tax, legal, or financial advice.
